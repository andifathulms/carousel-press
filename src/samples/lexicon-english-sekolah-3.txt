[cover kicker="SEKOLAH VS ASLINYA · #3"]
“What Is | Your Job?”
Nggak salah, cuma kaku. 5 kalimat sekolah dan versi santainya, dari kenalan sampai nanya jam.
---
[card kicker="Nanya kerjaan orang" number=off]
So, what do you *do*?
Di sekolah: “What is your job?”
Nggak salah, cuma kayak isian formulir. What do you do? artinya kerjanya apa, bukan lagi ngapain. Lengkapnya: What do you do for a living?
---
[card kicker="Nanya jam ke orang asing" number=off]
Excuse me, do you have *the time*?
Di sekolah: “What time is it now?”
Nggak salah. Versi sopan buat orang asing: Do you have the time? Awas, tanpa the (Do you have time?) biasanya artinya: ada waktu luang?
---
[card kicker="Atasan: “Did you send the report?”" number=off]
*Yes*, I sent it this morning.
Di sekolah: “Of course!”
Of course pas buat kasih izin: Can I borrow your pen? Of course! Tapi buat jawab info, bisa kedengaran “masa gitu aja nanya”.
---
[card kicker="Lapar banget" number=off]
I’m *starving*!
Di sekolah: “I am very hungry.”
Nggak salah, cuma kaku. Starving (informal) = lapar banget. Arti aslinya kelaparan parah, jadi pakai buat ngobrol santai saja.
---
[card kicker="Diajak makan siang bareng" number=off]
That *sounds good*!
Di sekolah: “Yes, I agree.”
Nggak salah, cuma kaku buat ajakan santai. Sound good = kedengarannya oke. Mirip: That sounds like a good idea.
---
[table]
Contekan: sekolah vs ngobrol
| Sekolah | Lebih santai |
| What is your job? | What do you do? |
| What time is it now? | Do you have the time? |
| Of course! (jawab info) | Yes, I sent it. |
| I am very hungry. | I’m starving! |
| Yes, I agree. | That sounds good! |
Versi sekolah tetap benar. Of course tetap pas buat kasih izin.
