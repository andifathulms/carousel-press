Thinked? Buyed? | Bukan Begitu
7 kata kerja yang V2-nya berakhiran -ought / -aught, dan bunyinya sama.
---
[table]
Pola *-ought* dan *-aught*
| V1 | V2 / V3 | Arti |
| think | th*ought* | berpikir |
| bring | br*ought* | membawa |
| buy | b*ought* | membeli |
| fight | f*ought* | bertarung |
| seek | s*ought* | mencari |
| teach | t*aught* | mengajar |
| catch | c*aught* | menangkap |
Seek lebih formal; sehari-hari biasanya look for.
---
Ejaan beda, bunyi sama
-ought dan -aught dibaca persis sama: thought, bought, taught, caught berakhir dengan bunyi "ot" panjang. Huruf gh-nya tidak dibaca.
---
[word]
pos: verb · V2/V3 of think
word: thought
ipa: /θɔːt/ (UK) · /θɑːt/ (US)
say: "thot", ujung lidah di antara gigi
meaning: berpikir; mengira
example: I *thought* you were coming.
translation: Kukira kamu mau datang.
---
[word]
pos: verb · V2/V3 of teach
word: taught
ipa: /tɔːt/ (UK) · /tɑːt/ (US)
say: "tot", o panjang
meaning: mengajar; mengajari
example: My mum *taught* me how to cook.
translation: Ibuku yang mengajariku masak.
note: Thought dan taught cuma beda bunyi awal: th dan t.
---
[word]
pos: verb · V2/V3 of buy
word: bought
ipa: /bɔːt/ (UK) · /bɑːt/ (US)
say: "bot", o panjang
meaning: membeli
example: I *bought* this shirt online.
translation: Kemeja ini aku beli online.
---
[compare]
wrong: Yesterday I thinked about it.
right: Yesterday I *thought* about it.
why: Think tidak pakai -ed. Begitu juga bukan buyed, teached, catched: yang benar bought, taught, caught.
---
[compare]
wrong-label: Salah arti
right-label: Tepat
wrong: I brought a new phone yesterday.
right: I *bought* a new phone yesterday.
why: Maksudnya membeli? Pakai bought, dari buy. Brought dari bring, artinya membawa. Beda satu huruf r, artinya beda jauh.
---
[end]
