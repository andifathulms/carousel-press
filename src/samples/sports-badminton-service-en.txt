The Badminton Serve, | Explained
Why so many serves get called as faults.
---
Hit it below 1.15 m
The whole shuttle must be below 1.15 metres from the court surface at the moment the racket hits it.
---
A fixed height since 2018
Judges used to compare the hit with the server's waist. BWF trialled a fixed height from March 2018 and made it permanent that year.
---
Base first, no spin
The racket must hit the base of the shuttle first. Since 2025, adding spin as you release the shuttle is not allowed.
---
Feet stay down
Server and receiver both keep part of both feet on the court, not moving, until the serve is hit.
---
Keep moving forwards
Once the serve starts, the racket must keep moving forwards. A pause after the backswing counts as undue delay.
---
Diagonal courts
Server and receiver stand in diagonally opposite service courts, without touching the lines.
---
Right or left?
In singles, serve from the right when your score is zero or even, and from the left when it's odd.
