[cover kicker="GRAMMAR ALA INDONESIA · #1"]
“Yesterday I Go to the Mall”? | Harusnya “I Went”
Kenapa kita sering lupa ganti kata kerjanya, dan cara gampang ingatnya. Plus 2 kuis kecil.
---
[card kicker="Mana yang benar?" number=off]
*A.* Yesterday I go to the mall. | *B.* Yesterday I went to the mall.
Teman kantor nanya kemarin kamu ke mana. Kamu jawab pakai yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[card kicker="Kenapa kita bilang “go”?" number=off]
Jawabannya B. | Di Inggris, *kata kerjanya* ikut berubah.
“Kemarin aku pergi ke mal”: pergi tetap pergi, kata kemarin yang menunjukkan waktunya.
Di Inggris, yesterday saja belum cukup: go harus jadi went. Namanya past simple.
---
[card kicker="Polanya" number=off]
Kejadian lampau: | kata kerja *bentuk lampau*
Kebanyakan cukup tambah -ed: worked, called, watched. Sebagian tidak beraturan, jadi dihafal: went, ate, spent.
Bentuknya sama untuk semua orang: I went, she went, they went. Kecuali be: was, were.
---
[compare]
wrong: Last night I watch a K-drama until 2 a.m.
right: Last night I *watched* a K-drama until 2 a.m.
why: Last night sudah bilang kapan, tapi watch tetap harus jadi watched. Kata kerja beraturan cukup tambah -ed.
---
[compare]
wrong: I spend too much at the mall yesterday.
right: I *spent* too much at the mall yesterday.
why: Spend jadi spent: d berubah jadi t. Cambridge menulis kesalahan ini di daftar kesalahan umumnya: We spend a lot of money yesterday.
---
[card kicker="Kuis 2" number=off]
*A.* Did you saw my message? | *B.* Did you see my message?
Chat kamu ke teman satu tim belum dibalas dari pagi. Kamu tanya pakai yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[compare]
wrong: Did you saw my message?
right: Did you *see* my message?
why: Kalau sudah ada did atau didn’t, kata kerjanya balik ke bentuk dasar: did you see, I didn’t see. Lampaunya sudah dibawa did.

[table]
Kemarin? Kata kerjanya *ikut lampau*
| Ala Indonesia | Inggris |
| Yesterday I go there. | Yesterday I went there. |
| I watch it last night. | I watched it last night. |
| I spend it yesterday. | I spent it yesterday. |
| Did you saw it? | Did you see it? |
| I didn’t went. | I didn’t go. |
Bentuk lampau sama untuk semua orang (kecuali was, were). Setelah did dan didn’t: bentuk dasar.
