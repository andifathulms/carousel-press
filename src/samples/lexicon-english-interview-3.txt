[cover kicker="INTERVIEW · #3"]
“Why Should We Hire You?” | Jawab Pakai Bukti
Pola 4 langkah buat fresh graduate dan yang pindah kerja, plus kalimat siap pakai.
---
[card kicker="Jawaban yang lebih kuat" number=off]
You need someone *good with numbers*, and I’ve done that work in my internship.
Yang lemah: “Because I really need this job.”
Nggak salah grammar-nya, tapi isinya tentang kamu, bukan tentang mereka. Yang dicari: apa yang bisa kamu bawa ke tim.
---
[card kicker="1 · Kebutuhan mereka" number=off]
From the job ad, you’re *looking for* someone who’s good with numbers.
Buka dengan yang mereka cari, ambil dari iklan lowongan. Good with = jago menangani sesuatu.
---
[card kicker="2 · Buktinya" number=off]
In my internship, I *was in charge of* the weekly sales report.
In charge of = bertanggung jawab atas. Satu contoh nyata lebih kuat dari “I’m a hard worker”. Ada angka? Sebut, asal benar.
---
[card kicker="3 · Nilai tambahmu" number=off]
*On top of that*, I can use basic SQL, so I can prepare the data myself.
On top of that = selain itu. Pilih yang bikin kamu beda, bukan skill yang semua pelamar punya.
---
[card kicker="4 · Penutup" number=off]
I’d *love to bring* that experience to your team.
Tutup dengan apa yang bisa kamu kasih ke tim. Boleh tambah: Does that match what you’re looking for?
---
[compare]
wrong: I am very interesting in this position.
right: I’m very *interested* in this position.
why: Interested = tertarik, perasaanmu. Interesting = menarik, sifat sesuatu: This position is interesting.
---
[card kicker="Jangan dipilih" number=off]
Tiga jawaban yang bikin rugi
“Because I really need this job.” Isinya tentang kamu. | “I’m clearly the best candidate.” Kedengaran sombong. | “I’m a hard worker.” Tanpa contoh, susah dipercaya.
---
[card kicker="Ringkasan" number=off]
Why should we hire you?
*Kebutuhan:* From the job ad, you’re looking for someone who’s good with numbers. | *Bukti:* In my internship, I was in charge of the weekly sales report. | *Nilai tambah:* On top of that, I can use basic SQL. | *Penutup:* I’d love to bring that experience to your team.
---
