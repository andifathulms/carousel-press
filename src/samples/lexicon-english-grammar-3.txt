[cover kicker="GRAMMAR ALA INDONESIA · #3"]
“I Very Happy”? | Kurang Satu Kata: “I’m”
Kata sifat butuh am, is, are. Plus kebalikannya: kapan are justru nggak boleh ada.
---
[card kicker="Mana yang benar?" number=off]
*A.* I very happy with the result. | *B.* I’m very happy with the result.
Atasan bilang presentasimu bagus. Kamu balas pakai yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[card kicker="Kenapa “am” sering hilang?" number=off]
Jawabannya B. | Inggris butuh *am, is, are*.
“Aku senang sekali”: senang langsung nempel ke aku, tanpa kata tambahan.
Di Inggris, kata sifat (happy, tired, late) perlu be di depannya: I’m very happy. Namanya linking verb.
---
[card kicker="Polanya" number=off]
I *am* · he/she/it *is* | you/we/they *are* + kata sifat
Kemarin: I/he/she/it *was*, you/we/they *were*.
I’m tired. She’s sick. We were late.
---
[compare]
wrong: Sorry, I late. The traffic was bad.
right: Sorry, *I’m late*. The traffic was bad.
why: Late (telat) itu kata sifat, jadi perlu am. Saat ngobrol atau chat, pakai bentuk pendeknya: I’m.
---
[compare]
wrong: My boss very angry yesterday.
right: My boss *was* very angry yesterday.
why: Kemarin? Be-nya ikut lampau: was untuk I, he, she, it; were untuk you, we, they.
---
[card kicker="Kuis 2: kebalikannya" number=off]
*A.* Are you live in Jakarta? | *B.* Do you live in Jakarta?
Kenalan sama teman baru di kampus. Kamu mau tanya dia tinggal di mana.
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[compare]
wrong: Are you live in Jakarta?
right: *Do you live* in Jakarta?
why: Kalau ada kata kerja (live, work, like), tanyanya pakai do atau does, bukan are. Are untuk kata sifat: Are you tired?
---
[table]
Kata sifat: *am, is, are*
| Ala Indonesia | Inggris |
| I very happy. | I’m very happy. |
| Sorry, I late. | Sorry, I’m late. |
| He angry yesterday. | He was angry yesterday. |
| Are you live here? | Do you live here? |
Kata sifat: am, is, are (kemarin: was, were). Kata kerja: tanpa be, tanyanya pakai do.
