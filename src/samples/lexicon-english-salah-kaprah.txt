11 Kalimat Inggris | yang Sering Salah
Banyak yang keliru di sini. Santai, ini wajar.
---
[compare]
wrong: I am agree with you.
right: I *agree* with you.
why: Agree itu kata kerja, jadi tidak perlu am. Sama seperti kita tidak bilang I am like.
---
[compare]
wrong: We discussed about the plan.
right: We *discussed* the plan.
why: Discuss langsung diikuti topiknya, tanpa about. Kalau mau pakai about: have a discussion about.
---
[compare]
wrong: Can you explain me the route?
right: Can you *explain the route to me*?
why: Explain tidak bisa langsung diikuti orang. Pakai to sebelum orangnya.
---
[compare]
wrong: She's married with a doctor.
right: She's married *to* a doctor.
why: Married to + pasangan. Tapi "married with two children" benar: sudah menikah dan punya dua anak.
---
[compare]
wrong: I very like this song.
right: I *really like* this song.
why: Very tidak dipakai langsung dengan kata kerja. Bisa juga: I like this song very much.
---
[compare]
wrong: I'm boring. Let's go out.
right: I'm *bored*. Let's go out.
why: Bored artinya merasa bosan. Boring artinya membosankan. Jadi I'm boring berarti "aku orang yang membosankan".
---
[compare]
wrong: According to me, this plan is risky.
right: *In my opinion*, this plan is risky.
why: According to dipakai untuk pendapat orang lain atau sumber: according to the report. Untuk pendapatmu sendiri: in my opinion atau I think.
---
[compare]
wrong: I've lived here since two years.
right: I've lived here *for* two years.
why: For untuk lamanya waktu: for two years. Since untuk titik awal: since 2022, since Monday.
---
[compare]
wrong: Your plan is more better.
right: Your plan is *better*.
why: Better sudah bentuk perbandingan dari good, jadi tidak perlu more. Sama halnya: bukan most biggest, tapi biggest.
---
[compare]
wrong: He entered in the room quietly.
right: He *entered* the room quietly.
why: Enter langsung diikuti tempatnya, tanpa in atau to. Untuk bus, jangan pakai enter: get on the bus.
---
[compare]
wrong: I was really surprise.
right: I was really *surprised*.
why: Untuk perasaan, pakai bentuk -ed: surprised, bored, interested. Tanpa -ed, surprise itu kata benda atau kata kerja.
---
[end]
