Handball, | Explained Properly
Not every touch is a foul.
---
Not every touch is an offence
The Law says it directly: not every touch of a player's hand or arm with the ball is an offence.
---
Where the arm starts
At the bottom of the armpit. A ball off the shoulder, above that line, is not handball.
---
Offence 1: on purpose
Deliberately touching the ball with your hand or arm. Moving your arm towards the ball is the classic example.
---
Offence 2: making yourself bigger
If your arm's position isn't justified by your body movement, you took a risk. Ball hits it? Offence.
---
Offence 3: scoring with it
Scoring straight off your hand or arm, or right after the ball touches it, is an offence. Even by accident.
---
A teammate's accident is fine
Since 2021, an accidental handball that sets up a teammate's goal is no longer an offence.
---
The punishment
A direct free kick, or a penalty in your own box. Deliberately stopping a goal with your hand is a red card, except a goalkeeper in their own box.
