Walked, Played, Wanted: | Bunyi -ed Ada Tiga
Dan cuma satu yang menambah suku kata.
---
[table]
Tiga bunyi *-ed*
| Bunyi | Contoh |
| t | walk*ed*, stopp*ed*, laugh*ed* |
| d | play*ed*, clean*ed*, call*ed* |
| id | want*ed*, need*ed*, decid*ed* |
---
Cara cepat mengingatnya
Kata dasarnya berakhir bunyi t atau d? Tambah satu suku kata: "id".
Selain itu, -ed tidak menambah suku kata. Walked dibaca satu ketukan, bukan wal-ked.
---
[word]
pos: verb · V2/V3 of walk
word: walked
ipa: /wɔːkt/ (UK & US)
say: "wokt", satu suku kata
meaning: berjalan kaki
example: I *walked* to school this morning.
translation: Tadi pagi aku jalan kaki ke sekolah.
---
[word]
pos: verb · V2/V3 of play
word: played
ipa: /pleɪd/ (UK & US)
say: "pleid", satu suku kata
meaning: bermain
example: We *played* football after class.
translation: Kami main bola sepulang kelas.
---
[word]
pos: verb · V2/V3 of want
word: wanted
ipa: /ˈwɒntɪd/ (UK) · /ˈwɑːntɪd/ (US)
say: "WON-tid", dua suku kata
meaning: ingin; mau
example: She *wanted* to quit.
translation: Dia pengin berhenti.
---
[compare]
wrong-label: Jangan
right-label: Begini
wrong: walk-ed (dua ketukan)
right: *walkt* (satu ketukan)
why: Bunyi k tidak bersuara, jadi -ed dibaca t dan menyatu dengan suku kata sebelumnya.

[end]
