[cover kicker="SALAH NGOMONG · #1"]
“He Said Me”? | Harusnya “He Told Me”
Say dan tell sama-sama berarti bilang, tapi polanya beda. 3 kuis kecil, jawabannya di slide sesudahnya.
---
[card kicker="Kuis 1" number=off]
*A.* He said me the news. | *B.* He told me the news.
Jawaban di slide berikutnya.
---
[compare]
wrong: He said me the news.
right: He *told me* the news.
why: Tell langsung diikuti orangnya: tell me, tell her. Say tidak. Kalau mau sebut orangnya, pakai say to: he said to me.
---
[card kicker="Kuis 2" number=off]
*A.* She told she was sick. | *B.* She said she was sick.
Jawaban di slide berikutnya.
---
[compare]
wrong: She told she was sick.
right: She *said* she was sick.
why: Tanpa orang yang diajak bicara, pakai say. Tell butuh orangnya: she told me she was sick.
---
[card kicker="Kuis 3" number=off]
*A.* Don't say a lie. | *B.* Don't tell a lie.
Jawaban di slide berikutnya.
---
[compare]
wrong: Don't say a lie.
right: Don't *tell* a lie.
why: Ini pasangan tetap, dan boleh tanpa orang: tell a lie, tell the truth, tell a joke, tell a story.
---
[table]
Ringkasnya
| Say | Tell |
| say something | tell someone something |
| say to someone | tell someone to … |
| “Hello,” she *said*. | tell a lie, tell the truth |
| say goodbye, say thank you | tell a joke, tell a story |
Untuk menyuruh pakai tell: They told us to come back the next day.
---
[word]
pos: verb · V2/V3 of say
word: said
ipa: /sed/ (UK & US)
say: “sed”, bukan “seid”
meaning: berkata, bilang (bentuk lampau say)
example: She *said* she would wait for us outside.
translation: Dia bilang dia mau nunggu kita di luar.
note: Say dibaca “sei”, tapi said dibaca “sed”. V2 tell: told /təʊld/ (UK) · /toʊld/ (US).
