Why Tennis Counts | 15, 30, 40
And what actually happens at deuce.
---
Points in a game
Love, 15, 30, 40, game. The server's score is always called first.
---
Deuce
At 40-all it's deuce. Now you need two points in a row: advantage, then game.
---
Six games win a set
First to six games, at least two clear. At 6-all a tie-break decides it, unless the event plays advantage sets.
---
The tie-break
Points count 1, 2, 3. First to seven, at least two clear, wins the game and the set.
---
Grand Slam final sets
Since 2022, all four majors play a first-to-10 tie-break at 6-all in the deciding set.
---
So why 15, 30, 40?
Nobody knows for sure. The scoring is medieval and comes from real tennis. A clock-face theory is popular, but historians doubt it.
---
And why not 45?
Early records do say 45. One theory: it got shortened to 40 because it's quicker to say.
