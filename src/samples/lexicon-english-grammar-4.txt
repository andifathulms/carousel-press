[cover kicker="GRAMMAR ALA INDONESIA · #4"]
“My Sister Work in Jakarta”? | Tambah Satu Huruf: “Works”
Kenapa he dan she bikin kata kerja dapat -s, dan kapan -s-nya justru hilang. Plus 1 kuis.
---
[compare]
wrong: My sister work in Jakarta.
right: My sister *works* in Jakarta.
why: Satu orang selain aku dan kamu (my sister, Pak Budi, he, she) bikin kata kerjanya dapat -s.
---
[card kicker="Kenapa -s sering hilang?" number=off]
*Kerja* nggak pernah berubah. | *Work* bisa jadi works.
“Aku kerja, dia kerja, mereka kerja”: kata kerjanya sama terus.
Di Inggris, untuk he, she, dan it, kata kerjanya dapat -s: I work, she works. Namanya present simple.
---
[card kicker="Polanya" number=off]
he/she/it + kata kerja *-s*
Biasanya cukup -s: works, likes. Akhiran ch, sh, ss, x: -es (watches). Konsonan + y: -ies (studies).
Tidak beraturan: have jadi has, go jadi goes, do jadi does.
---
[compare]
wrong: Our office have a big canteen.
right: Our office *has* a big canteen.
why: It juga ikut: kantor, hp, laptop. Have jadi has. Contoh Cambridge: The building has three rooms, bukan have.
---
[card kicker="Kuis" number=off]
*A.* Does she likes spicy food? | *B.* Does she like spicy food?
Mau ajak teman kantor makan seblak. Kamu tanya temannya dulu pakai yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[compare]
wrong: Does she likes spicy food?
right: Does she *like* spicy food?
why: -s cuma dipakai sekali. Kalau sudah ada does atau doesn’t, kata kerjanya polos: She doesn’t like it.
---
[compare]
wrong: My mom always cook rendang for Lebaran.
right: My mom always *cooks* rendang for Lebaran.
why: Kebiasaan juga pakai pola ini. Always, usually, every day tidak menggantikan -s-nya.
---
[table]
He, she, it: *tambah -s*
| Ala Indonesia | Inggris |
| My sister work there. | My sister works there. |
| It have a canteen. | It has a canteen. |
| Does she likes it? | Does she like it? |
| She not like it. | She doesn’t like it. |
| Mom always cook. | Mom always cooks. |
Does dan doesn’t sudah membawa -s-nya, jadi kata kerjanya polos.
