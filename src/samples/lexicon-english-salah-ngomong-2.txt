[cover kicker="SALAH NGOMONG · #2"]
“I’m Waiting You”? | Harusnya “I’m Waiting for You”
Nunggu, dengar, lihat, balas, bayar: lima kata kerja yang butuh kata depan. Plus 2 kuis kecil.
---
[card kicker="Mana yang benar?" number=off]
*A.* I’m waiting you in the lobby. | *B.* I’m waiting for you in the lobby.
Kamu sudah sampai duluan, terus chat teman. Tulis yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[compare]
wrong: I’m waiting you in the lobby.
right: I’m *waiting for* you in the lobby.
why: Kalau ada yang ditunggu, wait selalu pakai for: wait for the bus, wait for me. Tanpa yang ditunggu, wait saja: Wait a minute.
---
[compare]
wrong: I’m listening music.
right: I’m *listening to* music.
why: Kalau ada yang didengar, listen pakai to: listen to me, listen to this song. Tanpa objek, listen saja: Listen!
---
[compare]
wrong: Look this photo!
right: *Look at* this photo!
why: Kalau ada yang dilihat, look pakai at: look at me, look at the rain. Tanpa objek, look saja: Look!
---
[card kicker="Kuis 2" number=off]
*A.* Sorry, I’ll reply to your email tonight. | *B.* Sorry, I’ll reply your email tonight.
Balas email kantor yang telat. Pilih yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[compare]
wrong: Sorry, I’ll reply your email tonight.
right: Sorry, I’ll *reply to* your email tonight.
why: Reply + to + yang dibalas: reply to a message, reply to my chat. Mau tanpa to? Pakai answer: answer your email.
---
[compare]
wrong: I’ll pay the food.
right: I’ll *pay for* the food.
why: Barang yang dibeli pakai for: pay for the tickets. Tagihan atau orangnya tanpa for: pay the bill, pay the driver.
---
[table]
Ringkasnya
| Bahasa Indonesia | Bahasa Inggris |
| nungguin kamu | wait *for* you |
| dengerin lagu | listen *to* music |
| lihat foto ini | look *at* this photo |
| balas email | reply *to* an email |
| bayar makanan | pay *for* the food |
| bayar tagihan | pay the bill |
Tanpa objek, kata depannya hilang: Wait! Listen! Look!
