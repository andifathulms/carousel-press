“I'll Look Into It” | Artinya Mau Dicek
Bukan “aku lihat ke dalamnya”. Ini 10 phrasal verb yang sering muncul di kantor dan kampus.
---
[compare]
wrong: The client complained. We'll look it into.
right: The client complained. We'll *look into it*.
why: Look into nggak bisa dipisah. Objeknya selalu di belakang, termasuk it.

[word]
pos: phrasal verb · informal
word: figure out
say: “FI-ge aut”
meaning: akhirnya paham, atau menemukan jawaban setelah dipikir-pikir
example: I can't *figure out* why my code isn't working.
translation: Aku belum nemu kenapa kodeku nggak jalan.
---
[word]
pos: phrasal verb
word: follow up
meaning: menindaklanjuti; mencari tahu lebih lanjut
example: Thanks for the report. I'll *follow it up* tomorrow.
translation: Makasih laporannya. Besok aku tindak lanjuti.
note: Gaya US: follow up on something. Kata bendanya: a follow-up (tindak lanjut).
---
[word]
pos: phrasal verb
word: come up with
meaning: memikirkan atau menemukan ide, rencana, atau solusi
example: Rina *came up with* a great idea for the event.
translation: Rina punya ide keren buat acaranya.
---
[compare]
wrong: The form is ready. Please fill in it.
right: The form is ready. Please *fill it in*.
why: Kalau objeknya kata ganti (it, them, him), taruh di tengah. Sama seperti hand it in, set it up, figure it out.
---
[compare]
wrong: I keep putting off to call the bank.
right: I keep *putting off calling* the bank.
why: Setelah put off, kata kerjanya pakai -ing: put off going, put off doing.
---
