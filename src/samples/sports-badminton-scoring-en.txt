Badminton Scoring | Is About to Change
How it works now, and what changes in January 2027.
---
Best of three games
A match is the best of three games. That part stays the same.
---
Every rally scores
Win the rally, win a point, no matter who served. This is rally scoring.
---
Today: first to 21
At 20-all you need a two-point lead. At 29-all, whoever scores the 30th point wins.
---
Breaks and ends
A 60-second break when the leader reaches 11. In game three, players change ends at 11.
---
From 4 January 2027: first to 15
BWF members voted for it in April 2026. At 14-all, win by two. At 20-all, the 21st point wins.
---
What else moves
The mid-game break, and the change of ends in game three, move from 11 points to 8.
---
It has changed before
Until 2006, games mostly went to 15 and only the serving side could score. Rally scoring to 21 arrived in 2006.
