[cover kicker="GRAMMAR"]
"I Live Here Since 2020" | Belum Tepat
Since-nya sudah benar. Yang perlu diganti kata kerjanya, plus kapan pakai for.
---
[compare]
wrong: I live here since 2020.
right: I *have lived* here since 2020.
why: Mulai di masa lalu dan masih berlangsung sampai sekarang? Pakai present perfect: have/has + V3. Bentuk pendeknya: I've lived.
---
[table]
Since atau *for*?
| Since: sejak | For: selama |
| since 2020 | for six years |
| since Monday | for three days |
| since 8 a.m. | for two hours |
| since I was a kid | for ages |
Since + titik awal (kapan mulainya). For + lamanya (berapa lama).
---
[compare]
wrong: We've been waiting since two hours.
right: We've been waiting *for* two hours.
why: Two hours itu lamanya, bukan titik awal. Since tidak dipakai dengan jangka waktu.
---
[compare]
wrong: How long do you work here?
right: How long *have you worked* here?
why: Menanyakan lamanya sesuatu yang masih berlangsung? Pakai How long + present perfect. Jawabnya: For two years, atau Since 2024.
---
For juga untuk masa lalu
For tidak harus dengan present perfect. I *lived* in Bandung *for* four years artinya dulu pernah tinggal empat tahun, dan sekarang sudah pindah. Since tidak bisa dipakai di sini.
---
Since + kalimat
Setelah since boleh langsung kalimat dengan past simple: I've worked here *since I graduated*. She hasn't called *since she moved* to Surabaya.
---
[compare]
wrong: I've been learning English from I was in junior high.
right: I've been learning English *since* I was in junior high.
why: Kalau yang menyusul berupa kalimat (subjek + kata kerja) yang menunjuk titik awal, pakai since, bukan from.
---
[compare]
wrong: I have met him three years ago.
right: I *met* him three years ago.
why: Ago dipakai dengan past simple, bukan present perfect. Mau pakai present perfect? I've known him for three years.
---
[word]
pos: preposition · conjunction · adverb
word: since
ipa: /sɪns/ (UK & US)
say: "sins"
meaning: sejak; juga karena
example: *Since* it's raining, let's order food.
translation: Karena hujan, kita pesan makanan aja.
note: Arti karena ini sering muncul di email kerja dan tulisan.
