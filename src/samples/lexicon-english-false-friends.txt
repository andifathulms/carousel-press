Kata Inggris dan artinya
| Inggris | Artinya |
| actual | sesungguhnya |
| *current* | aktual, terbaru |
| sympathetic | pengertian, peduli |
| *likeable* | simpatik |
| sensible | masuk akal, bijak |
| *sensitive* | sensitif, peka |
Yang ditandai: padanan yang sebenarnya kita maksud.
---
[word]
pos: adjective
word: actual
ipa: /ˈæk.tʃu.əl/ (UK & US)
say: "EK-chu-el"
meaning: benar-benar ada; sesungguhnya
example: The *actual* price was higher than the ad.
translation: Harga sebenarnya lebih mahal dari iklannya.
---
[compare]
wrong-label: Salah arti
right-label: Tepat
wrong: Let's talk about actual issues.
right: Let's talk about *current* issues.
why: Aktual yang artinya hangat atau terbaru dalam bahasa Inggris: current atau latest.
---
[word]
pos: adjective
word: sympathetic
ipa: /ˌsɪm.pəˈθet.ɪk/ (UK)
say: "sim-pe-TE-tik"
meaning: peduli dan ikut merasakan kesusahan orang lain; pengertian
example: She was very *sympathetic* when my dad was sick.
translation: Dia sangat pengertian waktu ayahku sakit.
note: Simpatik (menarik hati) dalam bahasa Inggris: likeable atau pleasant.
---
[compare]
wrong-label: Salah arti
right-label: Tepat
wrong: He's very sensible, he cries at sad films.
right: He's very *sensitive*, he cries at sad films.
why: Sensitif itu sensitive. Sensible artinya masuk akal atau bijak: that's a sensible idea.
---
[word]
pos: noun
word: argument
ipa: /ˈɑːɡ.jə.mənt/ (UK) · /ˈɑːrɡ.jə.mənt/ (US)
say: "AA-gyu-ment"
meaning: pertengkaran, adu mulut; juga alasan untuk mendukung pendapat
example: I had a big *argument* with my brother.
translation: Aku bertengkar hebat sama kakakku.
note: Di KBBI, argumen artinya alasan; tidak ada arti bertengkar.
---
[compare]
wrong-label: Salah arti
right-label: Tepat
wrong: My uncle works in a shoe fabric.
right: My uncle works in a shoe *factory*.
why: Pabrik itu factory. Fabric artinya kain: cotton fabric = kain katun.
---
[word]
pos: verb
word: realize
ipa: /ˈrɪə.laɪz/ (UK) · /ˈriː.ə.laɪz/ (US)
say: "RI-e-laiz"
meaning: menyadari; juga mewujudkan (impian, rencana)
example: I didn't *realize* it was so late.
translation: Aku nggak sadar ternyata sudah selarut ini.
note: Realisasi di KBBI artinya perwujudan. Ejaan UK: realise.
---
[compare]
wrong-label: Salah arti
right-label: Tepat
wrong: This car is very economic.
right: This car is very *economical*.
why: Ekonomis (hemat) itu economical. Economic artinya berhubungan dengan ekonomi: economic growth.
---
[compare]
wrong-label: Salah arti
right-label: Tepat
wrong: I sent the package by expedition.
right: I sent the package by *courier*.
why: Jasa ekspedisi (kirim paket) itu courier. Expedition artinya perjalanan terencana, misalnya menjelajah daerah yang belum dikenal.
---
[compare]
wrong-label: Salah arti
right-label: Tepat
wrong: He's very consequent. He always keeps his word.
right: He's very *consistent*. He always keeps his word.
why: Konsekuen (teguh pada ucapan dan keputusan) lebih dekat ke consistent. Consequent artinya terjadi sebagai akibat dari sesuatu.
