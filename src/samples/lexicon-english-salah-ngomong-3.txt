[cover kicker="SALAH NGOMONG · #3"]
“Many Informations”? | Harusnya “A Lot of Information”
Information, advice, feedback: kata yang nggak pernah pakai -s. Plus 2 kuis kecil.
---
[card kicker="Mana yang benar?" number=off]
*A.* Can you send me more informations? | *B.* Can you send me more information?
Kamu chat HRD, minta detail lowongan. Tulis yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[compare]
wrong: Can you send me more informations?
right: Can you send me more *information*?
why: Information itu uncountable (nggak bisa dihitung), jadi tanpa -s dan tanpa many. Pakai a lot of, some, atau more.
---
[compare]
wrong: My mentor gave me some good advices.
right: My mentor gave me some good *advice*.
why: Advice juga uncountable. Mau hitung satu? Pakai a piece of advice: Can I give you a piece of advice?
---
[compare]
wrong: The teacher gave us many homeworks.
right: The teacher gave us *a lot of homework*.
why: Homework nggak pakai -s, dan pakai a lot of, bukan many. Begitu juga housework.
---
[card kicker="Kuis 2" number=off]
*A.* Thanks for all the feedbacks! | *B.* Thanks for all the feedback!
Atasan habis review presentasimu. Balas yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[compare]
wrong: Thanks for all the feedbacks!
right: Thanks for all the *feedback*!
why: Feedback (masukan, tanggapan) juga uncountable. Bisa: positive feedback, some feedback, a lot of feedback.
---
[compare]
wrong: How many luggages can I bring?
right: How much *luggage* can I bring?
why: Luggage uncountable, jadi how much. Mau hitung kopernya? How many bags, atau how many pieces of luggage. Di US lebih sering: baggage.
---
[compare]
wrong: We need new furnitures for the office.
right: We need new *furniture* for the office.
why: Furniture dan equipment sama-sama kumpulan barang, jadi tanpa -s. Satu barangnya: a piece of furniture, a piece of equipment.
---
[table]
Mau dihitung? Pakai *a piece of*
| Kata | Satu buah |
| information | a piece of information |
| advice | a piece of advice |
| luggage | a piece of luggage |
| furniture | a piece of furniture |
| equipment | a piece of equipment |
Tanpa angka cukup some, more, atau a lot of: some advice, a lot of feedback.
