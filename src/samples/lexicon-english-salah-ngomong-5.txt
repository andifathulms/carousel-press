[cover kicker="SALAH NGOMONG · #5"]
“I Have Ever Been to Bali”? | Cukup “I’ve Been”
Cara bilang pernah, sudah, belum, dan masih dalam bahasa Inggris. Plus 2 kuis kecil.
---
[card kicker="Mana yang benar?" number=off]
*A.* I have ever been to Bali. | *B.* I’ve been to Bali.
Teman kantor nanya kamu pernah ke Bali atau belum. Kamu jawab pakai yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[compare]
wrong: I have ever been to Bali.
right: *I’ve been* to Bali.
why: Pernah di kalimat positif cukup have been. Ever dipakai di pertanyaan, kalimat negatif, dan if: Have you ever been to Bali?
---
[compare]
wrong: Rina isn’t here. She has been to Jakarta.
right: Rina isn’t here. She *has gone* to Jakarta.
why: Been to = pernah ke sana dan sudah balik. Gone to = lagi pergi, belum balik. Makanya Have you ever been to…, bukan ever gone to.
---
[compare]
wrong: Are you already eat?
right: *Have you eaten yet?*
why: Sudah … belum? Pakai have + bentuk ketiga + yet. Have you already eaten? juga benar, tapi kesannya kaget: lho, kok udah?
---
[compare]
wrong: Sorry, I haven’t finished it already.
right: Sorry, I haven’t finished it *yet*.
why: Belum di kalimat negatif pakai yet, biasanya di akhir. Already dipakai di kalimat positif dan pertanyaan: I’ve already sent it.
---
[compare]
wrong: I’ve sent already the report.
right: I’ve *already sent* the report.
why: Already jangan diselipkan di antara kata kerja dan objeknya. Taruh setelah have, atau di akhir kalimat: I’ve sent the report already.
---
[card kicker="Kuis 2" number=off]
*A.* I haven’t found my keys yet. | *B.* I still haven’t found my keys.
Kunci motor hilang dari pagi, sudah kamu cari ke mana-mana. Mana yang lebih pas?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[card kicker="Jawaban kuis 2" number=off]
*Dua-duanya benar.* | B lebih pas.
Yet cuma bilang belum. Still … not ada rasa “harusnya udah dari tadi”.
Posisinya beda: yet di akhir kalimat, still sebelum haven’t.
---
[table]
Pernah, sudah, belum, *masih*
| Indonesia | Inggris |
| Aku pernah ke Bali. | I’ve been to Bali. |
| Pernah ke Bali? | Have you ever been to Bali? |
| Dia lagi ke Jakarta. | She’s gone to Jakarta. |
| Sudah makan? | Have you eaten yet? |
| Belum selesai. | I haven’t finished yet. |
| Masih belum ketemu. | I still haven’t found it. |
Already: setelah have, bukan sesudah kata kerja. I’ve already sent it.
