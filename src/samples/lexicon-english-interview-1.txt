[cover kicker="INTERVIEW · #1"]
“Tell Me About Yourself” | Bukan Minta Biodata
Pola jawaban yang rapi, plus 4 kalimat siap pakai buat fresh graduate.
---
[card kicker="Jawaban yang lebih kuat" number=off]
I’ve just finished my marketing degree, and I did an internship in *digital marketing*.
Yang kaku: “My name is Rina. I was born in Bandung in 2002. My hobbies are reading and travelling.”
Nggak salah, cuma kaku. Nama dan CV-mu sudah mereka pegang. Yang ditunggu: kamu sekarang apa, pengalamanmu apa, dan kenapa posisi ini.
---
[card kicker="1 · Sekarang" number=off]
I’m a recent *marketing graduate*.
Mulai dari posisimu sekarang. Sudah kerja? Pakai: I’m currently working as a social media admin.
---
[card kicker="2 · Pengalaman" number=off]
During my internship, I was *responsible for* our Instagram.
Pilih satu pengalaman yang nyambung sama posisinya. Bukan semua isi CV.
---
[card kicker="3 · Ke depan" number=off]
Now I’m *looking to* build a career in digital marketing.
Be looking to artinya berencana atau ingin. Sopan dan umum di interview.
---
[card kicker="4 · Penutup" number=off]
That’s why I’m *excited about* this role.
Tutup dengan alasan kamu melamar posisi ini. Jawabanmu jadi punya arah.
---
[compare]
wrong: I am graduated from a university in Bandung.
right: I *graduated* from a university in Bandung.
why: Graduate di sini kata kerja, jadi cukup I graduated, tanpa am. Atau pakai kata bendanya: I’m a graduate.
---
[card kicker="Berapa lama?" number=off]
Cukup di bawah 2 menit
Idealnya kurang dari 90 detik. Latih polanya, jangan hafal kata per kata biar nggak terdengar kayak membaca.
---
[card kicker="Ringkasan" number=off]
Tell me about yourself
*Sekarang:* I’m a recent marketing graduate. | *Pengalaman:* During my internship, I was responsible for our Instagram. | *Ke depan:* Now I’m looking to build a career in digital marketing. | *Penutup:* That’s why I’m excited about this role.
