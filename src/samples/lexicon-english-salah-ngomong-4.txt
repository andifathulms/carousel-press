[cover kicker="SALAH NGOMONG · #4"]
“Let Me to Introduce Myself”? | Hapus “To”-nya
Let, make, must, had better, would rather: langsung kata kerja, tanpa to. Plus 2 kuis kecil.
---
[card kicker="Mana yang benar?" number=off]
*A.* Let me to introduce myself. | *B.* Let me introduce myself.
Kamu buka presentasi di kelas atau di kantor. Pilih yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[compare]
wrong: Let me to introduce myself.
right: *Let me introduce* myself.
why: Let + orang + kata kerja dasar, tanpa to. Contoh di Cambridge: She let me look at the photos, bukan let me to look.
---
[compare]
wrong: My boss made me to work on Sunday.
right: My boss *made me work* on Sunday.
why: Make yang artinya maksa juga langsung kata kerja. Bedanya cuma di kalimat pasif: I was made to work on Sunday.
---
[compare]
wrong: You must to submit the report today.
right: You *must submit* the report today.
why: Must, should, can, could, will: semua langsung kata kerja dasar. Yang tetap pakai to: ought to dan have to.
---
[card kicker="Kuis 2" number=off]
*A.* Can you help me to carry this box? | *B.* Can you help me carry this box?
Teman lagi pindahan kos. Kamu minta tolong pakai yang mana?
Jawab A atau B dulu, baru geser.
---
[compare]
wrong: Can you help me carrying this box?
right: Can you help me *(to) carry* this box?
why: Kuis 2 jebakan: A dan B sama-sama benar. Help boleh pakai to, boleh tanpa to. Yang salah cuma bentuk -ing.
---
[compare]
wrong: It’s late. I’d better to go home.
right: It’s late. *I’d better go* home.
why: Had better (sebaiknya, biar nggak repot) juga tanpa to. Bentuk pendeknya ’d better.
---
[compare]
wrong: I’d rather to stay at home tonight.
right: *I’d rather stay* at home tonight.
why: Would rather (lebih milih) langsung kata kerja. Bandingkan dua pilihan pakai than: I’d rather walk than wait.
---
[table]
Langsung kata kerja, *tanpa to*
| Pola | Contoh |
| let + orang | Let me explain. |
| make + orang | She made me laugh. |
| must, should, can | You should rest. |
| had better | You’d better hurry. |
| would rather | I’d rather walk. |
Help boleh dua-duanya: help me (to) carry. Ought to dan have to tetap pakai to.
