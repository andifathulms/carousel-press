[cover kicker="SEKOLAH VS ASLINYA · #4"]
“What’s Up?” | “I’m Fine, Thank You.”
Nggak salah, cuma kurang nyambung. 5 kalimat sekolah dan versi santainya, dari sapaan sampai pesan minum.
---
[card kicker="Teman nyapa “What’s up?”" number=off]
*Nothing much.* You?
Di sekolah: “I’m fine, thank you.”
Nggak salah, cuma kurang nyambung. What’s up? di sini nanya ada apa atau lagi ngapain, sering cuma sapaan. Contoh di Cambridge: “What’s up?” “Nothing much.”
---
[card kicker="Pesan minum di kafe" number=off]
*Could I have* an iced tea, please?
Di sekolah: “I want an iced tea.”
Nggak salah grammar-nya. Tapi menurut Cambridge Grammar, I want terdengar sangat langsung dan bisa kesannya kurang sopan. Could I have …? lebih halus.
---
[card kicker="Minta tolong angkat barang" number=off]
Could you *give me a hand* with this box?
Di sekolah: “Please help me.”
Nggak salah, cuma kaku. Give someone a hand = membantu, contoh di Cambridge: Could you give me a hand with the table, please?
---
[card kicker="Minta orang nunggu sebentar" number=off]
*Hang on* a minute!
Di sekolah: “Please wait a moment.”
Nggak salah, tetap sopan buat tamu. Ke teman atau rekan kerja, hang on (informal) = tunggu sebentar.
---
[card kicker="Ketemu teman lama" number=off]
Hey! *Long time no see!*
Di sekolah: “I have not seen you for a long time.”
Nggak salah, cuma panjang. Long time no see dipakai pas ketemu orang yang sudah lama nggak kamu lihat. Santai, buat teman.
---
[table]
Contekan: sekolah vs ngobrol
| Situasi | Lebih santai |
| What’s up? | Nothing much. You? |
| Pesan minum | Could I have …, please? |
| Minta tolong | Could you give me a hand? |
| Minta tunggu | Hang on a minute! |
| Teman lama | Long time no see! |
Versi sekolah tetap benar. Please wait a moment masih pas buat tamu.
