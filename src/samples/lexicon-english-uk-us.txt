Di London, Pants Itu | Celana Dalam
Di Amerika, pants itu celana panjang. Ini kata sehari-hari yang beda antara Inggris (UK) dan Amerika (US).
---
[word]
pos: noun · beda arti UK/US
word: pants
ipa: /pænts/ (UK & US)
say: “pents”
meaning: UK: celana dalam | US: celana panjang
example: I spilled coffee on my *pants*.
translation: (US) Celanaku ketumpahan kopi.
note: Celana panjang versi UK: trousers.

[word]
pos: noun · beda arti UK/US
word: biscuit
ipa: /ˈbɪs.kɪt/ (UK & US)
say: “BIS-kit”
meaning: UK: biskuit | US: roti kecil bundar
example: Would you like a *biscuit* with your tea?
translation: (UK) Mau biskuit buat teman minum teh?
note: Biskuit manis versi US: cookie.

Antre: queue atau line?
UK: We had to *queue* for an hour. | US: We had to *wait in line* for an hour. | Dua-duanya artinya kami harus antre sejam.
