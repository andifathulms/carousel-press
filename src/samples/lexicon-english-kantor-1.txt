[cover kicker="KANTOR · #1"]
Laporan Belum Siap, | Deadline Besok
Cara bilang ke atasan dengan tenang: 4 kalimat minta tambahan waktu, plus beda by dan until.
---
[card kicker="Laporan belum selesai" number=off]
Just a *heads-up*: I’m a bit behind on the report.
Yang kaku: “Sorry, Sir. I cannot finish the report.”
Nggak salah grammar-nya, tapi berhenti di kabar buruk. Atasan butuh kabar lebih awal, tanggal baru, dan apa yang sudah jadi.
---
[card kicker="1 · Kabari lebih awal" number=off]
I wanted to give you a *heads-up* about the sales report.
Heads-up (informal) = kabar lebih awal biar orang bisa siap-siap. Kirim jauh sebelum deadline, bukan pas hari H.
---
[card kicker="2 · Sebut posisinya" number=off]
I’m a bit *behind on* it because the data came in late.
Behind on (US) atau behind with (UK) = telat dari jadwal. Satu alasan singkat cukup, nggak perlu cerita panjang.
---
[card kicker="3 · Tawarkan tanggal baru" number=off]
*Would you mind if* I sent it on Thursday morning?
Pola sopan minta izin: Would you mind if I + kata kerja lampau. Kalau dijawab “No, not at all”, artinya boleh.
---
[card kicker="4 · Kirim yang sudah jadi" number=off]
*In the meantime*, I can send you what I have *so far*.
In the meantime = sambil menunggu. So far = sampai sekarang. Atasan bisa cek arahnya lebih awal.
---
[compare]
wrong: I will send it until Thursday.
right: I will send it *by* Thursday.
why: By = paling lambat, cocok buat deadline. Until = terus sampai waktu itu: I’ll be in the office until six.
---
[card kicker="Ringkasan" number=off]
Minta tambahan waktu
*Kabari:* I wanted to give you a heads-up about the sales report. | *Posisi:* I’m a bit behind on it because the data came in late. | *Tanggal baru:* Would you mind if I sent it on Thursday morning? | *Yang sudah jadi:* In the meantime, I can send you what I have so far.
---
[end cta="Simpan"]
