Nulis "Your Welcome"? | Harusnya "You're"
Yang dimaksud you're welcome. Ini 5 pasangan kata yang bunyinya mirip dan sering ketuker di chat.
---
[compare]
wrong: Your welcome, bro!
right: *You're* welcome, bro!
why: You're = you are. Your = milikmu: your bag, your idea. Tes cepat: bisa diganti you are? Tulis you're.
---
[word]
pos: short form of you are
word: you're
ipa: /jʊə(r)/ · /jɔː(r)/ (UK) · /jʊr/ (US)
say: "yur" atau "yor"
meaning: kamu (itu/sedang)
example: *You're* doing great.
translation: Kamu hebat kok.
note: Di US, your dan you're dibaca sama persis. Di UK juga sering sama.
---
[compare]
wrong: My phone is old. It's battery dies fast.
right: My phone is old. *Its* battery dies fast.
why: Its (tanpa apostrof) = miliknya, untuk benda atau hewan. It's = it is atau it has.
---
[word]
pos: short form of it is / it has
word: it's
ipa: /ɪts/ (UK & US)
say: "its"
meaning: itu (adalah); itu sudah
example: *It's* been a long day.
translation: Hari ini panjang banget.
note: Its dibaca persis sama. Bedanya cuma di tulisan.
---
[table]
Their, there, *they're*
| Kata | Arti | Contoh |
| their | milik mereka | *their* house |
| there | di sana; ada | over *there* |
| they're | they are | *they're* late |
Ketiganya dibaca sama: "dheer". Bedanya cuma di tulisan.
---
[compare]
wrong: Their going to be late.
right: *They're* going to be late.
why: Bisa diganti they are? Tulis they're. Kalau maksudnya milik mereka: their car.
---
[compare]
wrong: My brother is taller then me.
right: My brother is taller *than* me.
why: Than untuk membandingkan (daripada). Then artinya lalu, kemudian, atau saat itu: We ate, then we left.
---
[word]
pos: preposition · conjunction
word: than
ipa: /ðən/ · /ðæn/ (strong form) · UK & US
say: "dhen" dengan e lemah
meaning: daripada
example: Jakarta is busier *than* Bandung.
translation: Jakarta lebih ramai daripada Bandung.
note: Then dibaca beda: e-nya jelas. Jadi bukan homofon, cuma ejaannya mirip.
---
[table]
To, *too*, two
| Kata | Arti | Contoh |
| to | ke; untuk | go *to* work |
| too | juga; terlalu | *too* spicy |
| two | dua | *two* coffees |
Too dan two dibaca sama, "tuu". To biasanya lebih pendek, kecuali ditekan.
---
[compare]
wrong: This sambal is to spicy for me.
right: This sambal is *too* spicy for me.
why: Terlalu = too, dengan dua o. Me too (aku juga) juga pakai too.
---
[end]
