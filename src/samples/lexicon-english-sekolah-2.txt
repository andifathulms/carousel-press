[cover kicker="SEKOLAH VS ASLINYA · #2"]
“Thank You.” | “You’re Welcome.”
Nggak salah, tapi bukan satu-satunya jawaban. 5 kalimat sekolah dan versi santainya.
---
[card kicker="Teman bilang “Thanks!”" number=off]
*No problem!*
Di sekolah: “You’re welcome.”
Nggak salah, tetap sopan. Buat bantuan kecil di obrolan santai, No problem atau No worries juga jawaban ramah untuk terima kasih.
---
[card kicker="Nanya arti kata" number=off]
What does *“deadline”* mean?
Di sekolah: “What is the meaning of deadline?”
Nggak salah, cuma kaku dan panjang. Pola pendeknya: What does … mean? Contoh di Cambridge: What does this word mean?
---
[card kicker="Pamitan sama teman" number=off]
Bye! *See you* tomorrow.
Di sekolah: “Goodbye.”
Nggak salah, cuma kedengaran resmi. Bye dan See you (later) itu versi informal yang biasa dipakai antar teman.
---
[card kicker="Mempersilakan tamu duduk" number=off]
Please, *have a seat*.
Di sekolah: “Sit down, please.”
Nggak salah, cuma kaku, kayak instruksi di kelas. Buat tamu atau klien: Have a seat atau Take a seat, artinya sama, silakan duduk.
---
[card kicker="Mau pulang" number=off]
I’m *heading home*.
Di sekolah: “I am going to go home.”
Nggak salah, cuma panjang. Head home artinya pulang, menuju rumah. Versi pendek lain: I’m going home.
---

Contekan: sekolah vs ngobrol
| Sekolah | Lebih santai |
| You’re welcome. | No problem! |
| What is the meaning of …? | What does … mean? |
| Goodbye. | Bye! See you. |
| Sit down, please. | Have a seat. |
| I am going to go home. | I’m heading home. |
Versi sekolah tetap benar. Untuk situasi resmi, masih aman dipakai.
