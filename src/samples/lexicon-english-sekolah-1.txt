[cover kicker="SEKOLAH VS ASLINYA · #1"]
“I’m Fine, Thank You, | And You?”
Nggak salah, cuma kaku. 5 kalimat hafalan sekolah dan versi santainya buat ngobrol beneran.
---
[card kicker="Ditanya “How are you?”" number=off]
*I’m good*, thanks. How are you?
Di sekolah: “I’m fine, thank you, and you?”
Nggak salah, cuma kedengaran kayak hafalan. Jawaban santai lain: Not too bad, atau Fine, thanks. Lalu tanya balik.
---
[card kicker="Kenalan sama orang baru" number=off]
Hi! *Nice to meet you.*
Di sekolah: “How do you do?”
Nggak salah, tapi How do you do itu salam formal, bukan nanya kabar. Jawabannya pun How do you do lagi, bukan I’m fine.
---
[card kicker="Sebut nama dan asal" number=off]
*I’m* Dimas. *I’m* from Medan.
Di sekolah: “My name is Dimas. I am from Medan.”
Nggak salah, cuma kaku. Di obrolan sehari-hari, bentuk pendek I’m yang biasa dipakai. My name is tetap benar dan sopan.
---
[card kicker="Nanya kesukaan orang" number=off]
Do you have any *hobbies*?
Di sekolah: “What is your hobby?”
Nggak salah, cuma kaku, kayak isian formulir. Mau lebih santai lagi: What do you like to do in your spare time? (spare time = waktu luang)
---
[card kicker="Nggak dengar jelas" number=off]
Sorry, could you *say that again*?
Di sekolah: “I beg your pardon?”
Nggak salah, sopan, tapi formal. Hati-hati: dengan nada kesal (terutama di UK), I beg your pardon! malah berarti kamu tersinggung.
---
[table]
Contekan: sekolah vs ngobrol
| Sekolah | Lebih natural |
| I’m fine, thank you. | I’m good, thanks. |
| How do you do? | Nice to meet you. |
| My name is Dimas. | I’m Dimas. |
| What is your hobby? | Do you have any hobbies? |
| I beg your pardon? | Could you say that again? |
Versi sekolah tetap benar. Untuk situasi resmi, masih aman dipakai.
---
[end cta="Simpan"]
