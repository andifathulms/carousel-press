## Catatan Kaki Sejarah

### sejarah-kutai-id: Kesultanan Kutai pernah dihapus
- Afriandi & Ariyadi, *Jurnal Reformasi* 10(2), 2020: https://jurnal.unitri.ac.id/index.php/reformasi/article/download/1919/pdf (UU Darurat 3/1953, UU 27/1959, 21 Jan 1960 handover, 22 Sep 2001 coronation)
- Sarip, *Yupa: Historical Studies Journal* 4(2), 2020: https://jurnal.fkip.unmul.ac.id/index.php/yupa/article/download/264/184 (1635 conquest of Muara Kaman, "ing Martapura")
- kaltimkece.id (Sarip): founding by Aji Batara Agung Dewa Sakti, palace now Museum Mulawarman, Sultan Aji Muhammad Arifin since 15 Dec 2018
- Left out: the year of the move to Tenggarong (often given as 1782, not verified); founding dates are traditional (*Salasilah Kutai*), so the deck says "menurut naskah".

### sejarah-yupa-id: Prasasti Yupa
- Sarip 2020 (above): seven yupa, Sanskrit in early Pallava script, 1879 and 1940 finds, Kudungga → Aswawarman → Mulawarman, no date on the stones, estimates c. 400 / 425 / 450 CE, name "Kutai" not in the inscriptions
- kaltimkece.id, "Menelusuri riwayat derma 20 ribu sapi": the 20,000 cows and archaeologists' doubt about the figure
- Museum Nasional Indonesia holds the originals.

### sejarah-syarif-kasim-id: Sultan Syarif Kasim II
- Wahyuni, Agustono & Warjio, "Siak Masa Revolusi (1945–1949)", *Yupa* 4(1), 2020: https://jurnal.fkip.unmul.ac.id/index.php/yupa/article/download/243/163 (3 Mar 1915, schools, 28 Nov 1945 telegram, 1946 Medan → Aceh, RRI, Rp20,000 + jewels)
- Setneg, Penerima Gelar Pahlawan Nasional 1959–2017: Keppres 109/TK/1998
- Disputed: "13 juta gulden" is widely repeated but no primary source was found; DJKN Kemenkeu says 10,000 guilders. The deck says the exact amount is unclear.
- Left out: the year the airport was renamed (not verified).

### sejarah-baabullah-id: Sultan Baabullah
- Indonesia Baik (Kominfo), profile of Sultan Baabullah: 1570, the siege, Portuguese gone by 1575
- Kompas English, 11 Nov 2020: reign 1570–1583, clove trade
- Setneg, 2020 National Heroes: Keppres 117/TK/2020
- Hanna & Des Alwi, *Turbulent Times Past in Ternate and Tidore* (1990), via Wikipedia
- Disputed: the exact 1575 date (15 July vs late December), so the deck gives the year only. "72 pulau" is a title from François Valentijn (1724), not a count.
- Left out: the exact day and killer of Sultan Khairun (only medium-confidence sources).

### sejarah-bongaya-id: Perjanjian Bongaya
- Sagimun M.D., *Sultan Hasanudin Menentang VOC* (Depdikbud, 1975), Wikisource Bab 4–5: 18 Nov 1667, Arung Palakka, war resumes 12 Apr 1668, Somba Opu falls 24 Jun 1669
- Atlas of Mutual Heritage (Nationaal Archief/RCE): Fort Ujung Pandang built 1630s, renamed Fort Rotterdam
- Treaty terms (monopoly, other Europeans out, forts razed except Somba Opu): Bassett 1958 and Ormrod & Rommelse 2020, cited via Wikipedia (medium confidence)
- Disputed: "Ayam Jantan dari Timur" as a Dutch title, no contemporary source; the number of articles in the treaty (24, 29 or ~30) is left out.

## Fourth pass: deeper, uncomfortable history (Oct 2026)

All ten history decks were extended to 12–14 slides. New claims and their main sources:
- **Kutai:** 1844 Murray episode and Dutch burning of Tenggarong (Tirto 2017 citing Irwin; Sarip & Nandini 2021); oil concessions from 1888 and royalties (Tirto citing Magenda; kaltimkece); 1950 Lapangan Kinibalu (kaltimkece 2018); 1964 arrests without trial and the attempted burning of the keraton, 21 May 1965 burning of regalia under Pangdam Soemitro (Muhammad Sarip, kaltimtoday 2025; detik 2025; kaltimkece 2018; sejarahkaltim.com); Rp64 juta and museum opening 25 Nov 1971 (kaltimkece; detik); RSJ Samarinda 1933 (RSJD Atma Husada Mahakam); Suaka Margasatwa Kutai 1936 (Mongabay 2013). Left out: "Koo Kutai"/Hirose, shouting "bakar", burned books, Nov 1965 release (Wikipedia only).
- **Yupa:** translations and readings (Chhabra 1965; Vogel, BKI 74, 1918; Kern 1880–82); 1879–80 handover by the Sultan via the Bataviaasch Genootschap (Notulen BG 1879–80); 20,000 vs 1,020 cows; the site dug by treasure hunters (Vogel citing Knappert, Von Dewall); Lesung Batu (Kasnowihardjo, Berkala Arkeologi 2006); 2023 fire (Museum Nasional statement). "Bukit Brubus" dropped.
- **Syarif Kasim II:** regency 1908–1915, 1916 and 1938 contracts, Japanese occupation, flight to Aceh 1947, allowance 1951–53, throne stolen 1967, crown (Yupa 4(1) 2020; Siti Zaqiah, UIN Jakarta 2024, citing ANRI and Delpher; Dutch SGD 1939–40; Historia.id 2024; Museum Nasional; Khairiah, Indo-Islamika 2024). "13 juta gulden" now shown as an unverified figure; "HIS pertama" claim dropped.
- **Baabullah:** Portuguese abuses before 1570, the murder of Khairun, the 1575 terms, Ternate's raids, Drake 1579 and Maria, 1583 death versions, 1606 and the VOC clove extirpation (Argensola 1609; Hakluyt; The World Encompassed 1628; Leirissa 2006; Hanna & Des Alwi 1990; Campo López 2017; Mostert/CAPASIA 2026). Corrected: not the first National Hero from Maluku Utara (Sultan Nuku, 1995); "70 islands" already in 1589.
- **Bongaya:** 1660 VOC attack, Gowa's forced labour of Bone people, the January 1667 Buton tragedy, treaty terms (250,000 ringgit, 1,000 slaves), starving Gowa in 1668, Somba Opu levelled, Karaeng Galesong (Sagimun 1975 incl. the treaty text; Andaya 1981; Palallo 2020).
- **Banjar:** succession and coal roots, Kalangan 1859, the Onrust (RCE wreck database; De Gids 1860), Dutch burning of Barito villages, Hidayatullah's capture by ruse, Demang Lehman (Kalimantan, Kemenpen 1953; Tirto 2021 citing Idwar Saleh and Helius Sjamsuddin; GNFI 2025).
- **Fort Rotterdam:** 1830 arrest at Magelang, Babad written in Manado (Carey 2008), "key of the East" (Keyzer 1862 via Sagimun), Ratnaningsih.
- **Kaltim names:** Balikpapan 1942 demolition and massacre (Indische Kamparchieven), 1945 battle (Australian War Memorial), Samarinda's administrative history and Tenggarong 1844 (Sarip & Nandini 2021).
- **Tambora:** selling children and self-enslavement, Zollinger's estimates, Bali and Lombok, slow recovery (de Jong Boers 1995).
- **Opu Daeng Risaju:** Andi Kambo, 23 Jan 1946 uprising incl. killings of pro-NICA people, 11 months without trial (Al-Qalam 28(2) 2022; Mattingaragau 2016; Masita dkk. 2022; Republika 2014). The torturer's name is left out (spelling unverified).

## Language decks (Oct 2026)

- **English Sehari:** IPA and meanings from Cambridge Dictionary (CALD) and Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, one dictionary per deck where they differ (e.g. US /θɑːt/ Cambridge vs /θɔːt/ OALD; the -ed deck uses OALD throughout). Usage rules: Cambridge Grammar ("discuss", "explain", "marry and divorce", "really", "past simple: pronunciation of -ed", "do or make?") and OALD notes ("discuss", "very", "actual"). "eventually" was dropped as a false friend (it's a Romance-language confusion, not an Indonesian one).
- **Kamus Kecil:** definitions quoted from KBBI VI Daring (kbbi.kemendikdasmen.go.id), including baku/tidak baku arrows. Portuguese origins from J. Oliveira, BiblioAsia 19(1), 2023 (Russell Jones 2007 not checked directly). Not in KBBI VI: surel, swastamita, sandyakala; KBBI's "email" entry means enamel. EYD Edisi Kelima for di/di-.
